Reporting handbook

Using Ledger

  1. Sign inUse the employee portal. Reporting administrators approve and export monthly summaries. Analysts normally rely on the assignment mail sent at the start of the close.
  2. Validate an importerAdministrators can run the legacy connectivity diagnostic before scheduling a transfer. The diagnostic accepts any hostname or IPv4 address listed for an importer endpoint.
  3. Archive rotationThe promotion queue is owned by the night-operations maintenance script, not by Ledger itself. Archive operators can inspect the active profile before staging reviewed reports.
  4. PublishOnly approved text reports are made available for download.
Security reminder: do not reuse reporting credentials outside their assigned service account. Night operators use a separate maintenance login and must never share it.
